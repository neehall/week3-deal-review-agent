VENDOR SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Vendor Services Agreement (the "Agreement") is made effective March 1, 2026, between
Cascade Logistics Inc. and Ferro & Wynn Supply Partners.

1. SCOPE OF SERVICES
Vendor shall provide warehousing and fulfillment services as described in the attached Statement
of Work. Pricing for these services shall be invoiced monthly per the rate card referenced in
Schedule B (rate card not attached to this excerpt).

2. TERM
This Agreement shall remain in effect for thirty-six (36) months from the Effective Date and shall
automatically renew for successive twelve (12) month periods unless either party provides notice
of non-renewal at least ninety (90) days prior to the end of the then-current term.

3. DISPUTE RESOLUTION
In the event of any dispute, controversy, or claim arising out of or relating to this Agreement,
the parties agree that such dispute shall be finally settled by binding arbitration in accordance
with the Commercial Arbitration Rules of the American Arbitration Association, and judgment on
the award rendered may be entered in any court having jurisdiction thereof. Each party waives
its right to a jury trial.

4. GOVERNING LAW
This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Illinois.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have executed this Agreement as of the Effective Date.
