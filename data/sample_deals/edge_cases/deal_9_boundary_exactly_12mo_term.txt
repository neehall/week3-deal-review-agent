SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Services Agreement is entered into as of July 1, 2026 (the "Effective Date"), by and
between Talon Consulting Group and Waverly Foods Inc.

1. SCOPE
Talon Consulting Group shall provide operational consulting services to Waverly Foods Inc. as
described in the attached Statement of Work.

2. FEES
Waverly Foods Inc. shall pay Talon Consulting Group a total fee of $96,000.00 for the term of
this Agreement, payable in equal monthly installments.

3. TERM
The term of this Agreement shall be exactly twelve (12) months from the Effective Date, after
which it terminates automatically with no further notice required from either party.

4. GOVERNING LAW
This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the State of Georgia.
