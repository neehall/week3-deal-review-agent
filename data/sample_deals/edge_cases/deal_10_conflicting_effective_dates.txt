AMENDED AND RESTATED LOAN AGREEMENT

This Amended and Restated Loan Agreement (the "Agreement") amends and restates in its entirety
the original loan agreement between Cobalt Peak Financial LLC ("Lender") and Driftwood Retail
Holdings ("Borrower") originally effective as of September 1, 2025.

1. PRINCIPAL AMOUNT
Lender agrees to loan Borrower the principal sum of $410,000.00.

2. INTEREST RATE
The Loan shall bear interest at a fixed annual rate of 11%.

3. EFFECTIVE DATE OF THIS AMENDMENT
This Amended and Restated Agreement is effective as of March 15, 2026, and supersedes the
original agreement referenced above in all respects.

4. TERM
The term of this Agreement shall be fifteen (15) months from the Effective Date.

5. TERMINATION
Either party may terminate this Agreement upon forty-five (45) days' written notice.

6. GOVERNING LAW
This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the State of Colorado.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have executed this Agreement.

Cobalt Peak Financial LLC                    Driftwood Retail Holdings
