MEZZANINE FINANCING AGREEMENT

This Mezzanine Financing Agreement is entered into as of August 12, 2026 (the "Effective Date"),
by and between Summit Capital Mezzanine Fund II ("Lender") and Palisade Hospitality Group
("Borrower").

1. PRINCIPAL AMOUNT
Lender agrees to advance Borrower a total of $2,000,000.00.

2. INTEREST RATE
The advance shall bear interest at a rate to be determined within the range of 16% to 21% per
annum, based on Borrower's trailing-twelve-month EBITDA at the time of each draw, as further
detailed in Schedule C (not attached to this excerpt).

3. TERM
The term of this Agreement shall be ten (10) months from the Effective Date.

4. GOVERNING LAW
This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the State of Florida.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have executed this Agreement as of the Effective Date.

Summit Capital Mezzanine Fund II                    Palisade Hospitality Group
