INTERNATIONAL SUPPLY AND FINANCING AGREEMENT

This International Supply and Financing Agreement is entered into as of October 3, 2026 (the
"Effective Date"), by and between Nordholm Maskin AB ("Supplier") and Continental Fabrication
Partners, Inc. ("Buyer").

1. FINANCED AMOUNT
Supplier agrees to extend trade financing to Buyer in the amount of EUR 275.000,00 (two hundred
seventy-five thousand euros), disbursed in three tranches.

2. FINANCE CHARGE
The financed amount shall accrue a finance charge of 12,5% per annum, calculated on the
outstanding balance.

3. TERM
The term of this Agreement shall be fourteen (14) months from the Effective Date.

4. TERMINATION
Either party may terminate this Agreement for convenience upon ninety (90) days' written notice
to the other party.

5. GOVERNING LAW
This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the Netherlands, without regard to its conflict
of law principles.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have executed this Agreement as of the Effective Date.

Nordholm Maskin AB                    Continental Fabrication Partners, Inc.
