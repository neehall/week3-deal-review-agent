LOAN AGREEMENT

This Loan Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of January 15, 2026 (the "Effective Date"),
by and between Northbridge Capital LLC ("Lender") and Alden Manufacturing Co. ("Borrower").

1. PRINCIPAL AMOUNT
Lender agrees to loan Borrower the principal sum of $250,000.00 (the "Loan").

2. INTEREST RATE
The Loan shall bear interest at a fixed annual rate of 9.5% (the "Interest Rate").

3. TERM
The term of this Agreement shall be twenty-four (24) months from the Effective Date.

4. TERMINATION
Either party may terminate this Agreement upon sixty (60) days' written notice, subject to
repayment of all outstanding principal and accrued interest through the termination date.

5. ARBITRATION
Any dispute arising under this Agreement shall be resolved through binding arbitration
administered by the American Arbitration Association, as set forth in the Arbitration Clause
attached as Exhibit A.

6. GOVERNING LAW
This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the State of Delaware.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have executed this Agreement as of the Effective Date.

Northbridge Capital LLC                    Alden Manufacturing Co.
