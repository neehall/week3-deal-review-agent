SERIES SEED TERM SHEET

This Term Sheet summarizes the principal terms of a proposed investment, effective as of
February 10, 2026, between Ridgeline Ventures LP ("Investor") and Pathframe Analytics, Inc.
("Company"). This Term Sheet is non-binding except for the Confidentiality and Governing Law
sections.

1. INVESTMENT AMOUNT
Investor agrees to invest a total of $1,200,000.00 in the Company's Series Seed Preferred Stock.

2. VALUATION
The pre-money valuation of the Company is $8,000,000.00.

3. TERM
This financing round shall close within six (6) months of the Effective Date, after which this
Term Sheet expires unless extended by mutual written agreement.

4. DISPUTE RESOLUTION
Any dispute relating to this Term Sheet shall be resolved by binding arbitration administered
by JAMS, as set forth in the arbitration clause in Section 4.

5. CONFIDENTIALITY
Both parties agree to keep the terms of this Term Sheet confidential.

6. GOVERNING LAW
This Term Sheet is governed by the laws of the State of California.
