COMMERCIAL LEASE AGREEMENT

This Commercial Lease Agreement ("Lease") is entered into as of June 1, 2026 (the "Effective
Date"), by and between Harbor View Properties LLC ("Landlord") and Solace Wellness Studio Inc.
("Tenant").

1. PREMISES AND RENT
Landlord leases to Tenant the retail space located at 220 Marina Way, Suite 4, for a total
annual rent of $84,000.00, payable in equal monthly installments of $7,000.00.

2. TERM
The term of this Lease shall be twelve (12) months, commencing on the Effective Date.

3. TERMINATION
Either party may terminate this Lease for cause upon thirty (30) days' written notice in the
event of a material breach that remains uncured after notice.

4. DISPUTE RESOLUTION
Any dispute arising under this Lease shall first be submitted to binding arbitration under the
rules of the American Arbitration Association before either party may pursue any other remedy.

5. GOVERNING LAW
This Lease shall be governed by the laws of the State of Oregon.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have executed this Lease as of the Effective Date.

Harbor View Properties LLC                    Solace Wellness Studio Inc.
