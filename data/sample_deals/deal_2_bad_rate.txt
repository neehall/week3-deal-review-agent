LOAN AGREEMENT

This Loan Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into by and between Summit Ridge Capital ("Lender")
and Vantage Retail Group ("Borrower"). The effective date of this Agreement is intentionally
omitted from this draft pending final signature.

1. PRINCIPAL AMOUNT
Lender agrees to loan Borrower the principal sum of $500,000.00 (the "Loan").

2. INTEREST RATE
The Loan shall bear interest at a fixed annual rate of 24.0% (the "Interest Rate").

3. TERM
The term of this Agreement shall be eighteen (18) months from the Effective Date.

4. GOVERNING LAW
This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the State of New York.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have executed this Agreement.

Summit Ridge Capital                    Vantage Retail Group
