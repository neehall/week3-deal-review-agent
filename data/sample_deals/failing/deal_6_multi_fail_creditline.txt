REVOLVING CREDIT LINE AGREEMENT

This Revolving Credit Line Agreement is entered into by Ironclad Capital Partners.

1. CREDIT LINE
Borrower may draw against a revolving credit line as needed. Amounts drawn shall be disclosed
separately and are not stated in this Agreement.

2. INTEREST RATE
Amounts drawn shall bear interest at a variable rate of Prime + 15%, currently equivalent to an
effective annual rate of 23.5%.

3. TERM
This Agreement shall remain in effect for thirty-six (36) months.

4. DISPUTE RESOLUTION
Any dispute shall be resolved through binding arbitration in a forum to be mutually agreed upon,
as described under the heading "Resolving Disagreements" elsewhere in this Agreement (see
attached rider, not included in this excerpt).

5. GOVERNING LAW
This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the State of Nevada.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the party has executed this Agreement.

Ironclad Capital Partners
