EQUIPMENT FINANCING LEASE

This Equipment Financing Lease is entered into as of April 3, 2026 (the "Effective Date"), by
and between Meridian Equipment Finance Corp. ("Lessor") and Blackwood Manufacturing LLC
("Lessee").

1. FINANCED AMOUNT
Lessor agrees to finance equipment for Lessee in the amount of $340,000.00.

2. FINANCE RATE
The financing shall carry an effective annual rate of 19.25%.

3. TERM
The term of this Lease shall be twenty (20) months from the Effective Date.

4. TERMINATION
Lessor may terminate this Lease immediately, for any reason or no reason, upon written notice to
Lessee. Lessee may not terminate this Lease prior to the end of the stated term under any
circumstances, and remains liable for all remaining payments even if the equipment is returned
early.

5. GOVERNING LAW
This Lease shall be governed by the laws of the State of Ohio.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have executed this Lease as of the Effective Date.

Meridian Equipment Finance Corp.                    Blackwood Manufacturing LLC
