MASTER DISTRIBUTION AND SUPPLY AGREEMENT

This Master Distribution and Supply Agreement (the "Agreement") is entered into as of
December 5, 2026 (the "Effective Date"), by and between Anchorline Industrial Supply Corp.
("Supplier") and Redcliff Distribution Partners LLC ("Distributor").

1. PRINCIPAL TERMS
Supplier agrees to extend financing to Distributor in the amount of $720,000.00. The
financing shall bear interest at a fixed annual rate of 8.75%. The term of this Agreement
shall be twenty-two (22) months from the Effective Date.

2. TERMINATION
Either party may terminate this Agreement upon sixty (60) days written notice for
convenience, or immediately for material breach that remains uncured for fifteen (15) days
after written notice.
